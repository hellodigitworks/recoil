What is held, and where from

Rebuild throws away the cached copy and pulls your whole history again. Disconnect forgets the session too, so you will have to log in to Whoop once more.

Apple Health

What Whoop cannot see

Steps, weight, what you logged eating and drinking, and any session Whoop missed. On your iPhone open Health, tap your photo, then Export All Health Data. Pick the zip it makes below. It is read on this phone and never leaves it.

Keep it safe

Back up your Health data

One file holding every day Recoil read from Apple Health. Save it somewhere real. If this browser ever forgets, or you want the same numbers on another device, this puts them back in a second instead of another ten-minute export.

Daily top-up

The morning Shortcut

Name the Shortcut you built, and Recoil can run it for you. It copies the day's numbers, you tap Take them. No browser opens and nothing leaves the phone.
